# Event Review: July 25

### Purpose of the Event
- Present the Hackathon & Product Development Competition
- Create a collaborative space and platform for developers, students, and tech enthusiasts to learn and hands-on build applications integrated with Agentic AI on AWS.
- Enable participating teams to practice, refine their ideas, develop prototypes (POC/MVP) within a short timeframe, and pitch their products to a professional panel of judges to win prizes.
- Knowledge Sharing & Inspiration from Industry Experts:
- isten to opening addresses and strategic insights from leading tech experts on the evolution of Artificial Intelligence, automated workflows, and career growth opportunities in the tech industry.
- Provide a stage for teams to present project outcomes, share practical development roadmaps, and receive feedback from both judges and the tech community.
- Foster a passion for technology, encouraging young talents to apply knowledge in practical settings, build real-world experience, and enhance their CVs with quality projects and achievements.
### Speakers

- **Joseph Marazota** – Head of Technology
- **One Team** – Hackathon Winner (1st Place)
- **Signal Scout** – Hackathon Runner-up (2nd Place)
- **Plan V** – Participating Team
- **3KA** – Participating Team
- **Six Pillars Team** – Participating Team

### Key Highlights

#### Keynote Address by Joseph Marazota
**Personal Journey & Appreciation for the Younger Generation**
- Expressed deep inspiration seeing young tech talents. Reflected on his career beginning 20 years ago, highlighting that young developers today have far greater opportunities, resources, and direct backing from AWS compared to the self-taught journey of the past.

**Shifting Tech Mindsets**:
Recalled early career anxieties about competing with senior engineers on legacy systems. Highlighted the paradigm shift: while legacy engineering focused on stability and minimal changes, today's Agentic AI and automation enable continuous deployments every minute.

**Message & Advice for Young Professionals**:
Encouraged youth to challenge traditional mindsets and conventional wisdom. Modern developers bring fresh approaches, learn faster, and are poised to lead the industry within 2–3 years. AI and automation are essential for scaling across all major global industries.

#### Presentations by the 5 Hackathon Teams
**One Team**:
- Shared their project: AI-Powered Conversational Ordering.
- Team Dynamics: Discussed managing a 5-member team with diverse English communication styles and backgrounds.
- Problem & Solution: Highlighted the complexities of conversational ordering and how brands lose customer engagement; presented their AI solution to bridge this gap.
- Demo: Conducted a live product demonstration of the winning solution.

**Signal Scout**:
- Shared their story of team formation and logo design.
- Discussed joining with limited prior info about the competition, driving forward with high enthusiasm despite physical fatigue.
- Highlighted the power of leveraging AI throughout their development workflow.
- Key Takeaway: Emphasized that "no matter how advanced, complex, or powerful your technology is, it cannot overcome the barriers of business logic and domain constraints."
- Demoed their product and presented core features.

**Plan V**:
- Described forming a team and brainstorming a project concept in a very short window, drawing directly from daily workplace experiences.
- Highlighted operational challenges faced by Solution Architects (SAs) and designed a product targeting both SAs and non-technical ("low-tech") users.
- Demonstrated their product prototype and summarized key technical learnings.

**3KA**
- Shared motivations, challenges, and benefits of competing in hackathons.
- Presented product demo and shared emotional takeaways from the journey.
- Advice to Peers: "Just register—don't wait or fear lacking skills. Experience and mindset matter most."

**Six Pillars Team**:
- Introduced team background, root problems identified, and how they developed their core idea.
- Presented product demo.
- Shared experience on task allocation, time management, and resolving execution bottlenecks.

### Lessons Learned

**AI and Agentic AI are Revolutionizing Software Development**: 
Beyond building traditional systems, developers must leverage AI Agents to automate workflows, accelerate delivery, and create intelligent products.

**Continuous Evolution of Tech Mindset**: 
As emphasized by Joseph Marazota, past standards become outdated quickly. Tech professionals must continuously learn, embrace change, and experiment with emerging technologies.

**Unprecedented Opportunities for the Youth**: 
Programs and hackathons supported by leaders like AWS provide essential environments for growth. Proactive participation is key.

**Business Logic Matters as Much as Technology**: As Signal Scout noted, technological capability must align strictly with real-world business needs to deliver true value.

**Teamwork is Crucial in Hackathons**: 
Effective task division, team collaboration, and mutual support under tight deadlines are critical success factors.

**Time Management & Prioritization**: 
Working under hackathon constraints sharpens planning abilities and performance under pressure.

**Embracing Challenges as Part of Growth**: 
Overcoming tight schedules, limited experience, and technical friction yields valuable practical expertise.

**Courage to Take Action**: As 3KA highlighted, waiting for perfection hinders progress. Taking initiative and gaining hands-on experience is the best path forward.
